
**During the beginning stages**, it is important to think deeply about the **overarching purpose** of the **specific monitoring activity**. This, in turn, will guide **subsequent planning about the specifics** of how monitoring will be conducted.  

Below are **some guiding questions** to prepare HIM: 

- **Why** is health inequality monitoring being done at this time?  
- What is the intended **impact** of monitoring?
- **Which policy cycles or windows of opportunity** are important to keep in mind?  
- **How often** should health inequality monitoring be done?  
- **Which groups of people** will use the knowledge generated from the monitoring exercise?  
- How can the interests and needs of **affected populations be understood and represented**?
- What are the **data requirements** for monitoring?  

Overall, **the goals of these questions** are to discuss the importance and purpose of health inequality monitoring, and describe who is involved, when it should be done, and how it is aligned within health information systems and other health sector activities.
