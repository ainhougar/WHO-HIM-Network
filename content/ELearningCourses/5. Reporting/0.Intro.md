
Reporting may be a **precursor** or may **be embedded in knowledge translation activities**. Reporting will **reflect aspects of all the previous steps** of the inequality monitoring cycle: (1) overarching purpose and scope of monitoring, (2)data sources, (3)key results. 

At this stage, a special focus given to: 
- contextualize findings
	-  Information about context, affected populations and findings from qualitative sources, if available, should be integrated. 
- data and results curation and interpreted. 

Importantly, reporting should not contribute to stigma or discrimination against population groups. 

Reporting formats: 
- Written reports
- Academic publications 
- Policy briefs
- Interactive displays 
- Presentation such as webinars, interviews and conferences
