
## Purpose
The general purpose of reporting is to raise awareness and share knowledge. 
To convey why reporting is being done it is important to share what the aims, goals and objectives are. All aspects of reporting should be aligned with the purpose. For outputs and results to be impactful, they reflect relevance with the purpose.

Purpose driven reporting ensures all aspects of reporting align with the stated purpose, including identifying an appropriate audience.  Members of the target audience should be consulted in the development of reporting outputs. 

Purpose describes: why reporting is being done 
Goal describes: specific outcome of reporting 
Objective describes: actions to achieve reporting goals 
Aim describes: the ideal that underlie reporting
## Audience 

Audience should be **positioned to support** the intended outcome of reporting. Reporting outputs should be developed in consideration of the audience (prior knowledge, skills, needs, interests).
- Does the audience have a predefined interest in the topic area? 
	- Can the audience help achieve goals and objectives of reporting? 

The audience may be quite targeted (ministry of health) or may encompass diverse groups of people (researchers, community leader, policy-makers, knowledge users). 

Understanding the audience: 
- Be familiar with the interests, needs and preferences of the target audience 
	- Ensure that reporting is relevant and accessible.

Type of reporting depending on the audience interests, needs and preferences: 
- Researchers and scientists: detailed technical contents
- Policy makers or programme managers: succinct summaries with clear and timely recommendations
- General audiences: plain language, focusing on new and relatable insights

In depth understanding may require seeking to know the audience's: 
- Prevailing biases or misconceptions 
- Ways of accessing information 
- Technical ability 
- Type of language (technical or plain) and level 
- Level of digital literacy 
- Relevant political, cultural and social influences 
- Process for using and sharing info 
- Potential sensitivities 


Member of the target should be involved in the development of reporting, outputs and approaches.
